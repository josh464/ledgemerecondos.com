Board of Governors

The volunteer Board of Governors can be reached through management. Submit a request on the portal, or call FirstService Residential, and your message will be passed along. The Board meets on the third Wednesday of each month. Owners who want to attend the open session must submit a request each time. See About Ledgemere.
