Good to know

Quiet hours

11 PM to 8 AM, every day. We share walls, so headphones help.

Fees due on the 1st

A $25 late fee applies to payments received after the 15th.

Winter parking ban

Nov 1 – Mar 31: no parking on the town roads from 1 AM to 6 AM, or at any time during a snowstorm.

No open flames

No charcoal grills, fire pits of any fuel type, tiki torches or other open flames anywhere in the community.

Holiday decorations

Up the day after Thanksgiving, down by January 31.

Ice dams

Please don't break off icicles; it damages the new roof shingles. Open a ticket instead.

Our community

Ledgemere Country is 388 homes in 93 buildings, set along wooded loops off Mountain Gate Road, Meeting House Path and Leland Farm Road in Ashland. The grounds include tree-lined drives, a pond and tennis courts, all cared for by the association since 1980.
