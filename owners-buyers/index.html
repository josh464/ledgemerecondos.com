Owners & Buyers

This page is for current owners, prospective buyers, and real estate and lending professionals.

Association fees and assessments

  • Monthly common-area fees are due on the 1st of each month. Payments received after the 15th incur a $25 late fee.
  • The Supplemental Condo Fee 2024-2028 is paid in three installments each year from 2024 through 2028, due July 1, August 1 and September 1.
  • If a delinquent account is sent to the association's attorney, the owner is responsible for the attorney's fees and collection costs, as Massachusetts law (M.G.L. c. 183A §6) allows.
  • Owners must be current on all fees, fines and assessments before submitting landscaping, building or other modification requests.

Fees fund the community's operating budget and reserves. See Finances & Budget for the current approved budget, and Resident Services for how to pay.

Insurance

  • The association carries a master insurance policy on the buildings and common areas. Certificates for lenders are available through the Resident Portal.
  • Owners should carry their own unit-owner (HO-6) policy for interior finishes, personal property, liability and loss assessment. Ask your insurance agent what coverage fits.
  • Promptly report any loss or damage to your unit or the common areas to management, and cooperate with any claim.
  • Don't do or keep anything that would raise the association's premiums or put its coverage at risk.
  • Fireplaces: the association's insurance requires owners with fireplaces to have their chimney inspected every 2 years and to submit the inspection report to management, through the Resident Portal.
  • Regular plumbing checks help avoid water claims, which affect the master policy renewal for everyone. See the Seasonal Guide.

Buying at Ledgemere

For buyers

  • Read the Rules and Regulations, especially parking, pets and exterior modifications.
  • Ask your agent for the resale package, which includes the governing documents and the unit's account status.
  • Once you close, register on the Resident Portal and fill out the census form.

For agents, attorneys and lenders

  • Resale packages, 6(d) certificates and mortgage questionnaires are ordered online from FirstService Residential using the unit's ZIP code, 01721.
  • Master policy certificates are available from management.
