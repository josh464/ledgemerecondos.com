About This Website

This website is published by the Ledgemere Country Condominium Association as a public, informational resource for residents, owners, prospective buyers and their agents.

Official documents govern

The plain-English summaries on this site are provided for convenience only. They don't change, replace or interpret the association's governing documents. In case of any difference, the recorded Master Deed, By-Laws, and Rules and Regulations, and decisions of the Board of Governors, govern. Town of Ashland regulations govern Town matters such as trash collection and parking on public roads.

Information on this site may change without notice. For the most current information, or for questions about your specific situation, contact management through the Resident Portal.

Privacy

This site doesn't collect personal information and doesn't list residents' names or contact details. Account information, payments and service requests are handled securely through the FirstService Residential Resident Portal.

Accessibility

We want this site to be usable by everyone. If you have trouble reading a page or a PDF, or need a document in another format, contact management and we'll help.
